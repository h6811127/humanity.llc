QR field kit

Physical internet rails for accountability: print a sticker, place it with consent, scan opens a case and one legal action — not a hit poster.

1 · Naming on print

  • Lead with Public Docket (partner / venue / press name).
  • Folk “Most Wanted” only if the nonviolence charter is on the same piece or clearly linked.
  • Never print FBI seals, red WANTED stamps, mugshot heroes, or agency chrome.

2 · What the QR opens

  • Prefer a case URL: /docket/{id}/ (example: /docket/netanyahu/) — that is the interim live_object scan path for starter cases.
  • Optional deep link to #action-kit so the first scroll ends in a legal civic action.
  • Optional steward upgrade: child-object /c/{profile}?q={qr} — see steward shell #qr-upgrade. Keep discovery off until C-v3.
  • Do not encode discovery pins or homepage URLs.

3 · Where to place

  • Teach-ins, chapter tables, partner venues that opted in — with steward present.
  • Café / print-shop boards only with owner consent.
  • Never private residences, never “how to find” maps, never materials that dox or stalk.

4 · Drop checklist

  1. Read the charter and ToS.
  2. Print with Public Docket eyebrow + short nonviolence line.
  3. Encode QR → case page (test scan on a second phone).
  4. Place only where you have permission.
  5. Log the drop (city, venue type, case id, date) — that log is a network good for Accountability Day.
  6. Offer the research kit beside the sticker.

5 · Copy you can print

Eyebrow: Public Docket
Line: Sourced counts · legal actions only · not law enforcement
Foot: Scan for evidence + one civic action this week

6 · Forbidden in the field

  • Violence, threats, celebration of injury or death.
  • Private addresses or confrontation tips.
  • Law-enforcement impersonation or poster-stamp chrome.
  • Polluting default stranger stickers with docket politics (branch, not center).
