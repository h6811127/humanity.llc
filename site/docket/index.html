    Nonviolence charter · how this list works
    • No violence, threats, stalking, or doxxing.
    • Allowed actions: petition, boycott, shareholder vote, FOIA, peaceful protest info, contact representatives, donate to legal aid or journalism, share verified sources.
    • ICC rows cite court allegations. Docket rows are campaign counts — labeled, not convictions.
    • Success = documented civic outcomes (policy, court process, divestment) — never harm to persons.
    • When a case closes — exit criteria published before new nominations expand.
    • ToS — violence, doxxing, threats, and LE impersonation are banned forever.

    We don’t overthrow people. We withdraw legitimacy from unjust power and build what deserves it.

    ToS · removal for violence-adjacent content

    Official Public Docket surfaces and tip / counter-docket submissions are removed — and submitters banned from docket tools — for:

    • Violence or threats — including “take out,” celebrating injury or death.
    • Stalking or doxxing — private addresses, “how to find,” non-public contact maps.
    • Harassment of private persons — recruiting tips to confront non-public figures.
    • Law-enforcement impersonation — fake warrants, agency seals, FBI / WANTED chrome.

    Corrections and good-faith rebuttals are welcome on each case page (counter-docket / corrections) — never via threats. Report abuse: info@humanity.llc.
