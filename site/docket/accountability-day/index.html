Annual ritual · outline

Accountability Day

Court of record + commons fair — not a burn rally. Celebrate documented outcomes and what chapters built. Never celebrate harm to persons.

Cadence

Annual per city or chapter. First pilot date TBD after legal review. Hosts use Public Docket naming on venue materials; folk “Most Wanted” only with the charter visible.

Program blocks

  1. Open — Charter read aloud · “We don’t overthrow people. We withdraw legitimacy from unjust power and build what deserves it.”
  2. Closed with receipts — Cases closed that year: show closed_kind + citeable outcome only.
  3. Corrections honored — Retractions and counter-docket replies that improved the record.
  4. Awards — Best sourcing · Most useful FOIA / public records · Strongest local chapter · Best network good shipped.
  5. Goods fair — Kits, translations, datasets built while campaigning — including the research kit and QR field kit.
  6. Close — One legal action of the week · invite next chapter hosts.

Forbidden

  • Celebrating death, injury, disappearance, or elimination framing.
  • Law-enforcement impersonation, fake warrants, or poster-stamp chrome.
  • Private addresses, stalking tips, or harassment of private persons.
