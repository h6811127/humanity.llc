Book of Weird Practices

For people with bodies

Optional experiments for attention, presence, and weird belonging. Steal them. Annotate them. Disagree. The scan page stays boring on purpose.

Oracles Body Commons On these rails

Oracles

Ask something that isn’t a feed.

  1. Ask a book

    Instead of asking a chatbot: open a physical book at random. Let your eyes land on the first sentence that catches you. Sit with it before you explain it away.

  2. Dream inbox

    Phone face-down overnight. On waking, write the first image before you open any feed.

  3. Library labyrinth

    Enter a library with no search query. Leave when a title calls. Check out one book you’re not ready for.

  4. Write what you won’t ask a machine

    One page — by hand if you can — of questions you keep for humans, books, or yourself. Keep it private unless you choose otherwise.

Body

Use the animal you live in.

  1. Mirror ten minutes

    Sit with your face in a mirror. Phone away. Let what arrives arrive — boredom, vanity, grief, joke — and stay until the timer ends.

  2. Ten minutes of silence

    No feed, no music, no optimizing the quiet. Sit. If nothing profound happens, that still counts.

  3. One phone-free errand

    Run one short errand with the phone off or left behind. Notice what you reach for when nothing pings.

  4. Salt the door

    After a hard day, leave a pinch of salt at the threshold. Sweep it out in the morning. Literal reset — no metaphor homework.

  5. Cold-water face

    Three full face dunks in cold water. No selfie. Come back to the room you’re in.

Commons

Practice with other people — or for them.

  1. Leave something useful

    A kit, a note, a working object for a stranger. No audience required. No streak.

  2. Repair as spell

    Mend one broken thing instead of replacing it. While you work, name what else you’re mending — out loud or on paper.

  3. Confess to a tree

    Say the thing outdoors, to a tree or open air. Leave no recording. Walk home.

  4. Circle of three

    Meet two others. Each shares one practice result. No advice unless someone asks.

  5. Anonymous blessing drop

    Leave something useful plus an unsigned note where a stranger will find it. Don’t post it.

On these rails

Same physical internet — stickers, status, vouch — as ritual, not as product homework.

  1. Return to a sticker

    Place a live QR on something real. Come back later and read what is true now — status changed, sticker stayed.

  2. Vouch only in person

    When you vouch, do it face to face under published rules. Not remote cosplay. Not a DM badge.

  3. Scan as omen

    Scan a live QR you’ve never met. Treat the current public status as the sentence of the day — then put the phone away.

  4. Sticker pilgrimage

    Walk until you find three public stickers or tags. Note what each place is claiming right now. No map app required if you can help it.

How this works · what it is not
  • This is a field guide, not a creed or spiritual doctrine.
  • Practices are optional. Skipping them does not fail membership or the product.
  • No scores, badges, or purity ladders. Share results if you want — not performance.
  • The public scan and card stay practical: current status, vouch, revoke — not vibes enforcement.
  • Fork, annotate, and disagree. Belonging here is revocable.
